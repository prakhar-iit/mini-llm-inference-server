Two paid sessions produce v1's numbers. Both use Llama-3.1-8B in bf16 and follow decision 25: the L4 checks
that everything runs and is correct, and every study number comes from the H100.

| Session | Instance | Purpose | Time (est.) | On-demand | Spot (us-east-1, 2026-11-14) |

| A. Smoke | g6.xlarge (1× L4 24 GB) | `pytest` incl. slow tests, M5b/M5c kernel checks, every study's smoke command | ~2–3 h | $0.80/h → ~$2 | $0.38/h (1c) → ~$1 |

## Spot: where and when
- **Region: us-east-1 only for now.** It is the only region with quota.
  - p5.4xlarge zones, cheapest first: 1c ($2.87), 1f ($3.90); the others are $4.67.
  - g6.xlarge zones: 1c ($0.38), 1a ($0.43). us-east-2b has the best g6 capacity score (9/10), but there's no
    quota in us-east-2.
- **Pick by capacity, not only price.** Before launching, check spot placement scores (`GetSpotPlacementScores`)
  and launch with an EC2 Fleet across 2–3 zones using `capacity-optimized`. On 2026-11-14 nearly every zone
  scored 1/10; recheck once the spot quota is raised.
- **When:** prices barely move by hour. Free capacity and interruption rates are better at US nights (about
  10 PM–6 AM Pacific) and weekends, so start session B then.
- **If no capacity** (`InsufficientInstanceCapacity`): try the next zone, then fall back to on-demand.

## Surviving interruptions (spot)
- AWS warns 2 minutes ahead at `http://169.254.169.254/latest/meta-data/spot/instance-action`. A watcher polls it
  and, on a warning, uploads `results/` to S3.
- After **every step** below, `aws s3 sync results/ s3://<bucket>/minillm/results/<session-id>/`.
- A rerun downloads what's in S3 and skips every step whose result files are already there.
- S4 is ~4 h on its own: run it **one rate point per call** with its own `--tag`, so an interruption costs one
  point, not the study. Check that `--compare` can merge split tags before session B.

**Where the time goes:**
- S2 sends 1,200 requests at each of 7 load levels, and a fixed rate means low levels take longest (ρ = 0.2
  alone is ~18 min).
- S4 sends 1,000 requests at each of 7 rates for each engine.
  - Rate 0.5/s takes ~33 min just to send.
  - M3 serves only ~1 request/s, so every higher rate becomes a ~15 min backlog to drain.
  - Running M3's top three rates with fewer requests would save ~1.5 h, but breaks decision 3's ≥ 1,000
    requests per point. Decide before the session.
